WHY THIS SPREAD

Diversified by design

01

Reduces concentration risk

No single sector carries the group. A slowdown in construction, for example, doesn't affect logistics or wellness demand.

02

Shares real infrastructure

Sectors that look unrelated on paper — appliances and construction, for instance — often share the same customer: a homeowner renovating.

03

Compounds operating know-how

Supply chain and logistics lessons learned in one sector get applied to the next, rather than relearned from scratch.
