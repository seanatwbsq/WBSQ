ABOUT WBSQ HOLDINGS

Built to compound, not just collect.

A Singapore-based holding company that owns and operates brands across home appliances, robotics, construction, logistics, and consumer wellness — sharing infrastructure across a growing portfolio.

OUR STORY

Why a holding structure

Most of WBSQ's brands solve a narrow, specific problem well — a fridge, a robot, a construction crew, a logistics run. What they don't have on their own is scale: the negotiating weight to get better supplier terms, the shared warehousing to cut fulfilment costs, or a second team to lean on when something breaks.

WBSQ exists to supply that scale without flattening what makes each brand work. Every venture keeps its own name, team, and customers. The holding company sits behind them, handling the parts that are more efficient done once — sourcing, logistics infrastructure, and back-office discipline — across all twelve.

HOW WE OPERATE

Three principles across every brand

01

Independent brands

Each business keeps its own name, customers, and operating team. WBSQ doesn't merge brands into a single identity.

02

Shared infrastructure

Sourcing, warehousing, and logistics are pooled across the portfolio, so smaller brands get enterprise-level terms.

03

Long-term ownership

WBSQ holds brands to grow them over years, not to flip them — accountability sits with the same team from year one onward.
