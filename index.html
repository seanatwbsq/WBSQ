ABOUT WBSQ

A holding structure built for compounding, not just collecting.

WBSQ Holdings is a Singapore-based holding company that owns and operates brands across home appliances, robotics, construction, logistics, and consumer wellness. Rather than running each business in isolation, we centralise sourcing, logistics, and operating know-how across the portfolio — so a lesson learned in one brand's supply chain shows up as an efficiency gain in another's.

Every brand keeps its own identity and team. What they share is the infrastructure behind them: procurement relationships, warehousing, and a Singapore-registered holding entity that backs every venture.
